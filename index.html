ZipMeta – Privacy Policy

@aykutsen1987 · Last updated: 2 November 2026

1. Controller

This policy applies to the ZipMeta Android app published by @aykutsen1987. Contact: aykutsen1987@gmail.com

2. Data we process

  • Device and ad data: Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, device model/OS, app version and ad impression/click data may be processed by ad partners.
  • Data kept on your device: Settings, preferences and in-app records are mostly stored only on your device. You are not asked to create a user account.
  • AI processing: When you use the related feature, the content you select/enter (text, image or audio) may be sent via our server to AI services (Groq). This data is used only to fulfil your request.
  • Server communication: Some features send HTTPS requests to our servers hosted on Render. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, request time).
  • Online features: When online/multiplayer or connected features are used, room/session info, moves and connection data and (if any) a nickname are relayed through our server.
  • Error data: Technical information may be processed to fix errors.

3. Permissions

Permissions are requested only for the related feature and can be turned off anytime in Android settings.
